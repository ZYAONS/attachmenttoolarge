Membership

The first 20 are founding members

The number of seats is our tribute to 20 MB. Founding members carry one extra mark on their card, and a note in the directory for as long as this Society shall exist.

What you receive

  • A membership number of the form ATT-20MB-000001
  • A printable membership card (framing is your own affair)
  • A place in the directory — name and number only, never an email address
  • The right to resign at any time, in one click, genuinely deleted, no copy kept
On security, plainly Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes; the plaintext never touches disk. Sessions are HttpOnly cookies. Every write needs a CSRF double-submit token. Registration and sign-in are both rate-limited per IP. All of this is genuinely implemented — the source is in server/server.mjs.
Extract from the statutes

Applying constitutes agreement to the following three articles

Article I

The original error outranks your paraphrase. When you file a ticket, paste the raw bounce message. Do not tidy it, and do not delete the lines that look useless.

Article II

Do not mock the person asking. The sole exception is file-naming habits — especially those who reach “final version” for the seventh time.

Article III

Cite a source and a date for every number. “I think it was 25 MB” is not data; it is a reminiscence.
